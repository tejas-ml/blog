About

Tejas Mahajan

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Central Florida, working in the Computational Biology Lab with Dr. Wei Zhang as a Graduate Research Assistant. I also serve as a Teaching Assistant in the Department of Computer Science. My expected graduation is 2031. I am based in Orlando, Florida.

My work focuses on single-cell multi-omics, machine learning, AI security, and quantitative methods, with an emphasis on computer science and systems fundamentals. Alongside research, I build research tools and systems software in Python, C++, and Rust.

This blog is for technical explanations of things I have built or am studying — how a method works, what tradeoffs it makes, and where it still falls short. It is not a publication list. For research software, manuscripts, and project demos, see my portfolio.
